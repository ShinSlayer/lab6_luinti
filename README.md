##What We Built

We built three circuits, one to power a light, one to open a door and other that stops flowing water and turns on a light when the water is flowing

The first circuit has two of the levers flowing into a not gate, these are the levers we don't want on. 
The not gate then flows into an and gate which checks if the levers we want on are on and the levers we want off are off

For the second circuit we did two and checks to make sure the levers we want on are on. Then checked if the lever we want off is off.
And when the checks are met it opens the door.

The third circuit has a lever going to a piston which stops the water flowing when turned on. And when the water flow is turned on it powers a light.
